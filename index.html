Veremark

Full Stack Developer

Veremark is a global background screening platform that employers use to run pre-employment checks: identity, criminal records, employment history and education verification, across 180+ countries and 40+ check types, with an API that plugs screening into hiring workflows. I worked on it as a full stack developer.

My scope on this product
  • REST APIs, Django + DRF. Endpoints that carry the whole check lifecycle, from a screening request coming in to the finished report going out.
  • Screening interfaces. The dashboards and report views hiring and screening teams work in every day, built on top of those APIs.
  • Third-party integrations. Connections to the external check and verification providers a screening platform depends on, with their results normalised into one clear report.
  • Background jobs. Long-running checks processed asynchronously, so a slow provider never blocks the person waiting on the result.
  • Data work. PostgreSQL models for candidates, checks and reports, designed so every result stays traceable end to end.

OTO

Full Stack Developer

OTO (OTO Capital) is a Bengaluru fintech, founded in 2018, that makes buying a two-wheeler easier: customers pick a bike, pay lower EMIs on a leasing-style plan run with bank and NBFC partners, and at the end of the term choose to own the bike or return it. I worked on it as a full stack developer.

My scope on this product
  • REST APIs, Django + DRF. The services behind the catalogue, plan and application flows, from picking a bike to signing the plan.
  • Customer interfaces. The browsing, plan-selection and application screens customers move through, responsive from a phone to a desktop.
  • Partner integrations. Bank and NBFC partner connections, the integrations financing decisions and plan status depend on.
  • Background jobs. EMI schedules, payment status sync and reminders processed asynchronously, so partner delays never freeze the customer flow.
  • Data work. PostgreSQL models for customers, bikes, plans and payments, kept consistent across every partner sync.
